Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

2nd Year English Guide (PEM) is an academic tool created by Adam Ameyoud for students at École Normale Supérieure de Bouzareah (ENSB). Your privacy is respected entirely.

Local Storage Only

All user data (grade inputs, module marks, and theme preferences) is stored strictly on your device using browser localStorage. No data is transmitted to remote databases or external servers.

Third-Party Services

The app loads fonts via Google Fonts. No analytical trackers, cookies, or telemetry libraries are embedded in this application.
