# 2nd Year English Guide (PEM) — ENS Bouzaréah

Official academic platform and course repository for **2nd Year English (PEM)** students at **École Normale Supérieure de Bouzaréah (ENSB)**.

Developed and maintained by **Adam Ameyoud** (`adam.ameyoud@ensb.dz` • WhatsApp: `0795523399`).

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Curriculum Modules & Course Files**: Structured syllabus materials for TEFL Didactics, Linguistics & Phonetics, British & American Literature, Advanced Grammar, Written Expression, Oral Expression & Phonology, and Translation Techniques.
- **Academic GPA Meter**: Interactive semester grade calculator using official ENSB coefficients (`TD × 0.2 + TP × 0.2 + Exam × 0.6`).
- **Official Announcements**: Live feed for department scheduling and notices.
- **Faculty Directory**: Contact emails for teachers and module coordinators.
- **Offline First**: All student grades and cached documents remain accessible without an active internet connection.
- **Android APK**: Standalone application for Android devices with custom collegiate icon.

---

## 📱 Mobile App (Android APK)
Students can download the official Android application package (`ENSB-English-Guide.apk`) directly from the web platform or from GitHub Releases.

---

## 🔐 Administrative Management
Administrative management is isolated in an external, session-authenticated portal (`admin-portal.html`) outside the student app.
Course materials, announcements, and faculty listings can be synchronized live with `data.json`.
